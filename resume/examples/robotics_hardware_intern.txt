Robotics & Hardware Prototyping Intern

Team:
Our hardware team designs and manufactures robotics products. We're seeking an
intern to support rapid prototyping and fabrication.

You will:
- Create 3D models in CAD (Tinkercad, Fusion 360) and run 3D printing jobs
- Support fabrication including laser cutting and additive manufacturing
- Assemble and test robotics kits (VEX robotics experience a plus)
- Help run STEM outreach and mentor students
- Iterate on prototype designs and document capabilities and limitations

Requirements:
- Hands-on prototyping and fabrication experience
- Comfort with 3D printing and CAD tooling
- Strong teamwork, organization, and communication
- Bonus: programming experience (Python or Java)
